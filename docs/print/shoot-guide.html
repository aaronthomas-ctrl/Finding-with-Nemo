Finding with Nemo: video shoot guide

Print this. DO is what you do on the page. SAY is what you speak. WHY IT WINS is the advantage the judge should notice. Target length 2:50. Hard limit 3:00.

The one-line story to open with: "When I went out to find customers, nothing told me what was around me. So I built Finding with Nemo: instant research and an agent that acts, in the field and in your own space, using NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius, with Tavily for the live web."

Use cases to name (you will say these, and show them briefly)

  • Job seekers: find nearby employers and what each company does.
  • Sales teams (the live demo): welding suppliers selling to fabricators and EPC contractors.
  • Procurement: shortlist nearby suppliers in minutes.

Before you press record (30 minutes)

  • On Render: Manual Deploy the latest image and wait for Live. Open /api/health: it must say OK.
  • Browser: Chrome, 1080p, zoom 100%, bookmarks hidden, notifications off, no keys or dashboards visible.
  • Sign in at finding-with-nemo.onrender.com. Use the organisation selector (top of left sidebar) to choose Steel Arc Welding Supplies. Press + in the chat to start a fresh conversation.
  • Dry-run every prompt once. The first request after idle is slow. Cut waits in the edit; never talk over a spinner.
  • For clip H: pick one large, real EPC contractor from your search results. Dry-run its research card first. Do not use invented demo clients for web research.
  • Voice clip: Chrome speech recognition may fail. Plan to paste the transcript, and say "the transcript is captured".
  • Your account must not hit its AI request cap (judges get 40 each). Keep yours high.
  • Open tabs in this order: Google Maps, the app, GitHub README. Switch tabs; never hunt for pages on camera.

"Request limit reached"Use your unlimited account, not the judge account.
Over 3:00Cut clips in this order: C, then K, then G. Never cut E, F, H or I.

Do not say

  • Anything about "watchers" or "predictions": they were removed from the app.
  • Speed or token numbers: the earlier measurements were affected by a bug and are not reliable.
  • "Blind test" for the 30 of 30 result. It is a regression score (those questions also helped us find bugs). Say "passed".
  • "Runs on a Nebius endpoint". The models run on Nebius Token Factory; the app is hosted on Render.
